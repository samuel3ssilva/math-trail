# Usage guide — getting started

A simple guide for anyone who has never used **Math Trail**. No jargon. In about 5 minutes you'll run your first activity.

> Read in Portuguese: [usage-guide.pt-BR.md](usage-guide.pt-BR.md)

---

## What it is (and what it isn't)

Math Trail helps **you, the parent**, run short math moments — 2 to 5 minutes — with a young child, using **real objects** (not the screen).

- **Who uses the app:** you. **The child never touches the phone.** They play with cubes, dinosaurs, dice, paper and pen.
- **It is not** a test, an exam or a diagnosis. **There is no "right" or "wrong" answer.** Skipping a day is completely fine.
- The app only **suggests** what to do, **explains how to run it**, and **keeps a record** so you can follow the rhythm — with no grades.

## What you'll need

Common household materials, depending on the activity:

- 🧩 snap cubes
- 🦕 a few toy dinosaurs (or any small counters)
- 🎲 one or two dice
- 📄 paper and pen

Each activity tells you exactly which materials to use.

## Open the app

- **App:** https://samuel3ssilva.github.io/math-trail/
- **Just want to look around first?** Open the **demo** with example (fictional) data: https://samuel3ssilva.github.io/math-trail/?demo=1
  The demo is only for exploring. When you're ready to start for real, use the app link (without `?demo=1`); it starts empty, with your own data.

**Language:** at the top right, tap **PT / EN** to switch between Portuguese and English. Everything changes language, including the activities.

## Set up in 30 seconds (optional)

Tap the gear **⚙** (top right) to open **Settings**:

- **Child's name** — optional. If you fill it in, the title shows the name; if you leave it blank, it reads "Math Trail". You can change it anytime.
- **Birth month** — used only to show the age in the header.
- **Current Kate Snow chapter** — if you follow the book *Preschool Math at Home*, pick the chapter and the app prioritizes activities on that theme. If you don't, leave it on "No chapter focus".

Tap **Save**, then **Close**.

## The four tabs

The tab bar moves you between:

| Tab | For |
|---|---|
| **Plan** | what to do now — the heart of the app |
| **Log** | recording how a session went |
| **History** | your sessions grouped by day |
| **Progress** | a skill trail and the week's rhythm (never grades) |

## Running your first activity

### 1. Pick a moment

On the **Plan** tab there are three daily windows — **Morning**, **Afternoon** and **Bedtime**. They are **optional opportunities**, not obligations: do one, several or none. A short activity already counts. A small coloured dot marks each window, and a **"now"** tag points to the window for the current time.

### 2. Choose an activity

In any window you can:

- **Choose from the list** — tap "choose activity" and select one; or
- **Ask for a suggestion** — tap the purple **✦** button. The app suggests a fitting activity.

### 3. Read the "how to"

A card appears with:

- the **materials** and rough duration;
- a **setup diagram** (how to arrange the objects on the table);
- a **parent script** — step by step of what to say to the child;
- **why** that activity was suggested.

### 4. Run it with the child (off-screen)

Do the activity following the script. To time it, tap **Start** — a small bar shows the elapsed time. When you're done, tap **End**.

Remember: **there is no "bad" outcome**. The goal is the moment together, not getting it right.

## Logging the session

After you end it (or anytime, from the **Log** tab), you record it — in three quick choices:

1. **How did it go?** — Full · Partial · Refused.
2. **Child's mood** — Excited · Neutral · Resisted (an **observation**, not a grade).
3. **How did the challenge feel?** — Too easy · Just right · Too hard. This helps the app tune the level next time.

If you like, open **"More details (optional)"** to note what sustained the activity (Play · Connection · Treat) and a short **note**. Tap **Save session**.

> A session you've ended is **kept safely** until you save it — if you close the app first, it isn't lost.

### Not a good moment?

Tap **"Not a good moment today"**. There's no penalty — the app respects rest.

## Following along (no pressure)

- **History** shows sessions grouped by day. You can edit or delete a session; the app recomputes everything on its own.
- **Progress** shows a skill trail, the milestones being built and mood over time — always as **plain counts**, never as grades or performance percentages.

## Install as an app and use offline

Math Trail is a **PWA**: you can install it to your phone's home screen (from the browser menu, "Add to Home Screen" / "Install"). Once opened once, it **works without internet** — the data stays on your device.

## Your data and privacy

- Everything stays **only on your device** (in the browser). No account, no server, no tracking.
- **Backup:** in Settings, **Export JSON** saves a file with your data; **Import JSON** restores from a file. Handy when changing devices.
- **Delete all sessions** clears the app's data on that device (asks for confirmation).

## Quick questions

- **Does the child have to get it right?** No. There's no right or wrong — it's play and togetherness.
- **Do I have to do all three windows every day?** No. They're optional. A short activity already counts.
- **I lost internet mid-session.** No problem — the app works offline.
- **I switched phones.** Export the JSON on the old one and import it on the new one.
- **Does this assess my child?** No. It's a tool for **you** to plan and remember the moments, without judgment.
